The Noraneko42A is 42key Alice keyboard.  

## Parts  

Top plate --- 1 pc  
Middle plate --- 1 pc   
Main FR4 plate --- 1 pc  
Bottom1 plate --- 1 pc    
Bottom2 plate --- 1 pc  
M2 spacer 3mm --- 9 pcs  
M2 screw 3mm --- 18 pcs  (Screw heads should be 2 mm or less）  
RP2040-Zero --- 1 pc   
1N4148W --- 42 pcs   
SK6812MINI-E --- 42 pcs  
MX key switch --- 42 pcs   
MX keycap 1U --- 35 pcs  
MX keycap 1.25U --- 2 pcs  
MX keycap 1.75U --- 3 pcs  
MX keycap 2U --- 2 pcs  

Bumpon --- Any quantity 

## Firmware
![IMG_7002](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/5214078/201300486-a19fce27-7261-4fac-a14e-f837b712de54.jpeg)
Firmware
[v3.0](https://github.com/darakuneko/Noraneko/raw/main/noraneko42a/v2.0/firmware/noraneko421_vial.uf2)  
Check if the firmware can write to RP2040-Zero.  
Download the firmware.   
Connect while holding down the Boot button.  
RPI-RP2 will appear.  
Copy the downloaded firmware to RPI-RP2.  
Make sure that Noraneko42A is connected to your PC.    
[Vial Support](https://get.vial.today/)
